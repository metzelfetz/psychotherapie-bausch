The core of my therapeutic work is **modern cognitive behavioural therapy** – a solution-focused approach that starts directly from your current life situation. Depending on your individual concerns, I complement this core with emotion-focused and experiential methods and with proven mindfulness, acceptance and relaxation techniques.

### Clinical focus:

- Depression & existential crises (e.g. burnout, chronic exhaustion)
- Anxiety disorders & panic disorder
- Obsessive-compulsive disorder
- Trauma-related disorders

### Specialised therapies ("third wave"):

- **Schema therapy (after J. Young):** For understanding and lastingly changing deep-seated patterns in life and relationships that have been shaped by your personal history.
- **CBASP (after J. McCullough / E. Schramm):** A highly specific method for treating chronic depression through the targeted analysis of interpersonal interactions.
- **Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT, after S. Hayes):** An approach that combines radical acceptance of what cannot be changed with the courage to actively align your life with your personal values.
